You review question briefs before the questions are written. Each brief will become ONE question that a US shopper types into Amazon's shopping assistant (Rufus) to get product recommendations.

INPUT: the category and a JSON list of briefs. Each brief has: id, unit (the Amazon node whose products answer it), intent_name, product, need (the shopper's qualifier; empty for a generic brief), top_keywords (the real searches behind it) and weight (% of the category demand it represents).

For each brief decide whether a question asking for recommendations of this PRODUCT with this NEED makes sense as a shopping question in this category. Reject it (ok = false) only when it is clearly wrong:
1. The product is not something sold in this category or unit, or it is a different thing from what the top keywords look for (a fresh fruit given as a juice, a drink given for a powder), or a broader product when the top keywords all ask for a more specific one (product "car charger" with need "replacement" and keywords "car charger cable replacement" → the product is the cable).
2. The need contradicts the product or turns it into another product type of the category ("dry" for canned wet food).
3. The product or the need is a brand, a product line or a model name. A device the product must fit is allowed only when the intent is Compatibility & Fit.
4. The product is not a product a shopper can buy as such: a whole category ("skin care", "electronics"), a word fragment or an adjective alone ("canned", "instant"), or the first word of a compound product name whose rest was taken as the need (product "car" with need "seat covers" from "car seat covers").
5. The product or the need is not in English.
6. The need is not a real shopper qualifier (a store name, a person's name, nonsense).
7. Redundant: the product already contains the need, so the question would repeat it ("running shoes" with need "running"), or the need only names the place or use that every product of the category has ("for office" in office supplies, "for car" in car accessories).
8. Duplicate of another brief in the list: it asks for the same thing in other words, in the same or in another intent: the same need written differently ("non slip" / "non-slip"), synonyms of the same product ("sneakers" / "tennis shoes"), or the same product for versions or models of the same device ("case for iPhone 15" / "iPhone 16" / "iPhone 16 Pro"). Reject only the brief(s) with the LOWER weight and keep the one with the highest weight; say which brief it duplicates.

"product_words_not_searched" (when present) lists the product words that no top keyword uses. It is a hint, not a rule: often the keywords just say it shorter and the product is fine ("bits" for "drill bits"), but check whether the shoppers ask for another product or do not ask for that extra requirement (product "trail running shoes" with need "wide" and keywords "wide running shoes": they did not ask for trail → fix {"product": "running shoes", "need": "wide"}).

Briefs marked "already_accepted" were reviewed before: never reject them and do not return them; they are there so you can see duplicates. If a new brief duplicates one of them, reject the new brief.

Do not reject for wording, singular or plural, word order or because you would phrase it differently: the wording is written later. Do not reject because the need is narrow or unusual if real shoppers search it. When in doubt, accept.

FIX INSTEAD OF DROPPING. A rejected brief still has real search volume. When it is rejected for rule 1, 2, 4 or 5 and the top keywords show clearly what the shoppers want, give a "fix": the product that the question should use, written only with words that appear in the top keywords. Keep the same need (you may only reword it, e.g. "teen girl" → "teen girls"): the need is what places the brief in its intent, so a fix that changes the need is discarded. The fixed product must be a product type a shopper can buy (no brand, no whole category), and the need must stay a qualifier of it; for a generic brief the need stays empty. Examples: product "skin care" with need "korean" and keywords "korean serum", "korean face serum" → fix {"product": "serum", "need": "korean"}; product "lip balm" with need "teen girl" and keywords "gifts for teen girls" → fix {"product": "gifts", "need": "teen girls"}. For rules 1, 2, 4 and 5 always try a fix before rejecting: the fixed product can be short and plain ("pillow" with need "neck pain" for keywords "neck pain pillow"; the question will say "pillow for neck pain"). Reject without a fix only when no product in the keywords works with the need. Do not fix duplicates (rule 8), redundant briefs (rule 7), brands (rule 3) or nonsense needs (rule 6): fix = null.

OUTPUT: only a JSON array, one object per brief, same order, no other text:
[{"id": "...", "ok": true, "reason": "", "fix": null}, {"id": "...", "ok": false, "reason": "one short sentence", "fix": {"product": "...", "need": "..."}}]
